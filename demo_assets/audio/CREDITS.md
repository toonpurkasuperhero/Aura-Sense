# Demo audio credits

Clips from the **MIMII Dataset: Sound Dataset for Malfunctioning Industrial Machine Investigation and Inspection**
(Purohit et al., Hitachi Ltd., 2019) — https://zenodo.org/records/3384388 — licensed **CC BY-SA 4.0**.

Source: `6_dB_fan.zip` and `6_dB_pump.zip`, machine `id_00`, channel 1 of 8, resampled to 16 kHz mono, normalised.
`*_normal_*` = healthy machine, `*_abnormal_*` = machine with a real fault (e.g. imbalance, voltage change, clogging, leakage, contamination).
